## 📌 Course Overview

This course focuses on using AI as a creative partner for generating ideas, organizing thoughts, and turning early ideas into practical plans. It explores how AI can help with brainstorming, exploring different possibilities, defining goals, and creating structured plans for projects and everyday tasks.

The course emphasizes working with AI interactively instead of expecting one perfect answer from a single prompt.

---

## 🎯 Key Learning Outcomes & Modules

### 1. Generate Ideas with AI

* Learned how to use AI to quickly generate and explore different ideas.
* Practiced using prompts to encourage creative and varied responses.

### 2. Explore and Develop Ideas

* Learned how to take an initial idea and develop it into more detailed possibilities.
* Used AI to examine different approaches and perspectives.

### 3. Organize Information

* Practiced using AI to organize unstructured thoughts and information.
* Turned brainstorming results into clearer categories and priorities.

### 4. Define Goals

* Learned how to use AI to clarify objectives and break larger goals into smaller steps.
* Explored ways to make plans more specific and actionable.

### 5. Create Action Plans

* Used AI to turn ideas into structured plans, tasks, and next steps.
* Learned how to refine plans through an iterative process.

### 6. Improve Ideas Through Collaboration

* Practiced treating AI as a brainstorming partner rather than simply an answer generator.
* Learned to review, question, and improve AI-generated ideas.

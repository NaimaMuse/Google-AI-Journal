## 📌 Course Overview

This course focuses on using AI to support research, information gathering, and the discovery of useful insights. It explores how AI can help investigate questions, organize information, identify patterns, and turn research into meaningful conclusions.

The course also highlights the importance of checking information and using human judgment when working with AI-generated results.

---

## 🎯 Key Learning Outcomes & Modules

### 1. Use AI for Research

* Learned how AI can assist with researching topics and answering complex questions.
* Practiced creating clear research prompts.

### 2. Gather and Organize Information

* Used AI to collect, summarize, and organize information.
* Learned how to structure large amounts of information into useful categories.

### 3. Ask Better Research Questions

* Learned how well-defined questions can improve research results.
* Practiced refining questions to obtain more relevant information.

### 4. Identify Patterns and Insights

* Explored how AI can help identify connections, trends, and important observations.
* Learned to move from raw information toward useful insights.

### 5. Evaluate AI Results

* Learned that AI-generated information should be reviewed and verified.
* Practiced thinking critically about the accuracy and relevance of AI responses.

### 6. Turn Research into Action

* Learned how research findings can be transformed into summaries, recommendations, and next steps.
* Used AI to communicate research findings clearly.

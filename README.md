## 🧠 What I Learned

Throughout the certificate, I learned how AI can support different stages of learning, problem-solving, creativity, analysis, and software development.

### 🤖 Understanding AI

I learned the basic concepts behind generative AI and Large Language Models and how to work with AI more effectively.

### 💡 Brainstorming & Planning

I learned how to use AI as a thinking partner to generate ideas, organize information, define goals, and create practical plans.

### 🔎 Research & Insights

I explored ways AI can support research, organize information, summarize findings, and help identify useful insights.

### ✍️ Writing & Communication

I learned how AI can help create, edit, organize, and adapt written communication for different audiences and purposes.

### 🎨 Content Creation

I explored how generative AI can support creative work, including developing ideas, creating content, and working with different media formats.

### 📊 Data Analysis

I learned how AI can assist with organizing data, identifying patterns, interpreting information, and communicating analytical findings.

### 💻 App Building

I explored how AI can assist throughout the software development process, from planning an application to generating code, debugging, testing, and improving features.

### 🚀 App Deployment

I learned about using AI during the final stages of application development, including preparing, configuring, troubleshooting, and deploying applications.
